Privacy Policy

Color Plunge! · Last updated 16 August 2026

Color Plunge! is a mobile game developed and operated by Jacob Marx (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the game and its associated tools collect, why, and what control you have over it.

Information the game collects

  • Gameplay data stored on your device — scores, progress and settings. This stays on your device unless you explicitly share it.
  • Basic technical information such as device model, operating system version and crash diagnostics, used only to fix bugs and improve performance.

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or microphone or camera access.

Our TikTok integration

We operate an automated tool that publishes gameplay clips of Color Plunge! to our own TikTok account. If you authorise a TikTok account through our tool, the following applies:

  • We request only the permissions needed to publish: basic profile information (user.info.basic) and video publishing (video.publish, video.upload).
  • We receive and store an access token and refresh token so the tool can post on a schedule. These are held as encrypted repository secrets and are never written to logs, shared, or transmitted to any third party.
  • We use the connection only to upload videos we created ourselves. We do not read your feed, your messages, your followers, or any other account content.
  • We do not collect, store, or process personal data belonging to any other TikTok user.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell or rent personal information.
  • We do not share personal information with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not build advertising profiles or track you across other apps and websites.

Third-party services

Distribution and payment are handled by the Apple App Store and Google Play, each under its own privacy policy. Our TikTok posting tool interacts with TikTok's Content Posting API, governed by TikTok's own privacy policy and terms.

Data retention and deletion

  • Gameplay data lives on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.
  • TikTok access tokens are deleted when the connection is revoked. You can revoke it at any time in TikTok under Settings → Security & permissions → Manage app permissions; the tokens stop working immediately.
  • To request deletion of anything else we may hold, email us at the address below and we will action it within 30 days.

Children's privacy

Color Plunge! is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete personal information we hold, and to object to certain processing. Email us and we will respond within the period your local law requires.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be announced in the app.
